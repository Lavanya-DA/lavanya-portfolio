Fake Job Posting Analysis-Fraud Pattern Analysis

01 · Business Problem

Identify patterns associated with fraudulent job postings and determine which posting characteristics may signal higher fraud risk.

02 · Analysis Approach

Conducted EDA on 17,079 job postings, cleaned inconsistencies, handled data-quality issues using Excel and MySQL, and developed a Power BI dashboard.

Key Findings

16.21% No company logo
9.83% Part-time roles
8.66% Remote roles
25.12% Fraudulent posts with salary

03 · Outcome

Identified three prominent fraud indicators and uncovered a notable difference in salary-listing behaviour between fraudulent and genuine postings.
